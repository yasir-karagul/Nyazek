# Admin Panel Pages: Finance and Risk

**Authoritative for:** the content and actions of the finance and risk pages of the Admin Panel.
**Read with:** [README](README.md) §2 · [navigation](navigation.md) §3 · [admin-panel](../05-trust/admin-panel.md) · [flows](flows.md) FL-13 to FL-15 · [sitemap](sitemap.md) §8.7–8.8
**Rules live in:** [ledger-reconciliation](../04-finance/ledger-reconciliation.md) · [payments](../04-finance/payments.md) · [commission-wallet-payouts](../04-finance/commission-wallet-payouts.md) · [tax-invoicing](../04-finance/tax-invoicing.md) · [fraud-risk](../05-trust/fraud-risk.md)

No page on this list edits a balance or a ledger entry. Money changes only through the documented operations (refund, adjustment, payout, reversal), each with its controls. Amounts are shown from the ledger (DAT-4).

---

## 1. Finance

| ID | Template | Content | Actions | States and guards | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AD-FIN-TX | `TPL-LIST` | Payment-side transactions: date, type (payment, refund, COD remittance, payout, chargeback), method and provider, provider reference, order or seller, amount, status. Filters by type, method, status, date; totals for the filter | Open the linked order, payout or refund; export | – | [payments](../04-finance/payments.md) §4 |
| AD-FIN-LEDGER | `TPL-LIST` | Tabs: Trial balance (account, debit, credit, balance at a date) · Account ledger (entries of one account with sub-ledger filter: seller, courier, PSP) · Journal (entries with their lines, posting rule ID and source event) · Periods (open, closed) | Drill from balance to entries to the source object; export; close a period (Finance Manager) | Read-only; closing a period is audited and irreversible in the panel | LED-1 to LED-7, posting rules |
| AD-FIN-RECON | `TPL-QUEUE` | Tabs: Daily summary · Exceptions · Sign-off. Summary per day and source (each PSP, each courier, bank): expected, received, matched, difference. Exceptions: type, amount, age, source line and platform record side by side. Invariant-check results | Import a settlement file; match manually; resolve an exception with a reason (creating an adjustment request where money moves); sign off the day | There is no tolerance (REC-1): every difference is an exception. Signing off a day records the exceptions still open, which stay assigned; a payout run cannot be approved until the previous business day is signed off (REC-6). An invariant failure shows a blocking notice and pauses payouts | REC-1 to REC-6, ledger invariants |
| AD-FIN-FUNDING | `TPL-DASH` | Cash by account (PSP balances, courier receivables by age, operating and trust bank accounts) against obligations (seller payables available and pending, refunds due, payouts in transit); the surplus or shortfall; forecast of the next payout run | Open the underlying list for any figure | Shortfall blocks creating a payout run and says by how much | Treasury requirement, PO-3 |
| AD-FIN-REFUNDS | `TPL-QUEUE` | Tabs (refund states, payments §4.3): Awaiting destination (`AWAITING_DESTINATION`) · To pay manually (`PROCESSING`, manual route) · In progress (`REQUESTED`, `PROCESSING` by API) · Needs review (`FAILED`, `MANUAL_REVIEW`, `RETURNED`) · Completed (`SUCCEEDED`, `CANCELLED`). Columns: refund number, order, customer reference, amount, destination type and masked details, source (cancellation, return, dispute), age against the target | Mark a manual transfer as sent with its reference and proof (second person confirms above the threshold); retry a failed automatic refund; request a new destination from the customer; cancel a refund created in error (dual control) | Destination details revealed only to the Finance roles, logged | PAY-14 to PAY-17 |
| AD-FIN-PAYOUTS | `TPL-LIST` | Payout runs: cut-off, sellers, total, status (`DRAFT`, `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED`, `EXECUTING`, `COMPLETED`, `COMPLETED_WITH_FAILURES`, `CANCELLED`; PO-11), created and approved by. "New run" per FL-15 | Create a run; submit for approval; execute or download the bank file; upload confirmations | Blocked by a funding shortfall, an unsigned reconciliation or an invariant failure, each stated | PO-1 to PO-11, REC-6 |
| AD-FIN-PAYOUT | `TPL-DETAIL` | One run: per-seller lines (amount, destination masked, status, bank reference), exclusions with reasons, totals, approvals, files | Open a seller's statement; record a payout as executed with the bank reference, or failed or returned with the bank's reason (PO-6) | Failed and returned payouts move to `RETRY_SCHEDULED` for the next run; there is no retry inside a run | PO-6, PO-7, PO-11 |
| AD-FIN-FAILED | `TPL-QUEUE` | Failed payout lines: seller, amount, reason, attempts, since | Contact the seller to fix the destination; the payout joins the next run once the destination is confirmed (`RETRY_SCHEDULED`) | – | PO-6 |
| AD-FIN-ADJ | `TPL-LIST` and `TPL-FORM` | Adjustments: seller, direction, amount, reason code, linked object, requester, approver, status. Form: seller, credit or debit, amount, reason code, explanation, evidence, linked order or case | Request an adjustment (always goes to approval) | An adjustment needs a linked object or a written justification; the form shows the wallet effect before submission | [commission-wallet-payouts](../04-finance/commission-wallet-payouts.md) §7, JE-31, DC-1 |
| AD-FIN-INV | `TPL-LIST` | Commission invoices: number, seller, period, amount, tax, issued date | Download; reissue a copy | Numbers are sequential and never edited; corrections are credit notes | PO-8, INV-N rules |
| AD-FIN-DOCS | `TPL-LIST` | Receipts and credit notes: number, type, order, issuer (seller or Nyazek), amount, date. Search by number or order | Download; view the linked order | – | [tax-invoicing](../04-finance/tax-invoicing.md) §3–5 |
| AD-FIN-LOY | `TPL-DASH` | Points outstanding and their liability value, earned and redeemed by period, expiry forecast, breakage | Export | P1 | [loyalty](../02-commerce/loyalty.md) §1 |

## 2. Risk

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AD-RISK-QUEUE | `TPL-QUEUE` | Tabs: Orders on hold · Accounts flagged · Seller signals. Columns: object, score, top factors, amount, time to target | Claim and open | – | RSK-5, RSK-6 |
